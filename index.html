The Premier Competitive Team

MLBA Elite

MLBA Elite is our tryout-based tournament program — the premier competitive identity for strong players from Moses Lake and surrounding communities. Elite is earned through tryouts, not guaranteed by purchasing another program. Team fees vary by tournament and team costs.

For Coaches, Teams & Organizations

Home of Columbia Basin AAU

MLBA administers the Columbia Basin AAU local league and registration operation. Most Moses Lake AAU teams operate under the Columbia Basin AAU club umbrella.

🏀

League Administration

We manage league registration, administration, team participation, and scheduling support across the Columbia Basin.

🤝

Area Teams Welcome

Surrounding-area teams register to participate in the league and MLBA-operated tournaments. Boys and girls teams may carry their own team names.

🗓️

Tournaments

MLBA organizes tournament participation for teams across the region. Fees vary by event.
