Who We Are

More Than a Team

Moses Lake Basketball Academy (MLBA) has grown into the basketball organization serving Moses Lake and the surrounding communities. What began as skills training is now a complete program: a girls player-development Academy, the tryout-based MLBA Elite competitive teams, and administration of the Columbia Basin AAU local league.

Our mission is community-driven and player-first. We meet athletes where they are, build real fundamentals and confidence, and give every committed player a clear pathway to keep growing — from the Academy, to league teams, to Elite.
