Our Role

Running the League

MLBA administers the Columbia Basin AAU local league and registration operation. Most Moses Lake AAU teams operate under the Columbia Basin AAU club umbrella.

We manage league registration, administration, team participation, scheduling support, and tournament participation for teams across the region.
