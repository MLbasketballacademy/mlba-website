Tryout-Based Selection

Earned, Not Purchased

MLBA Elite is our premier competitive team identity. Rosters are formed through tryouts — Elite participation is earned and is not guaranteed by purchasing another program. Academy participants receive an invitation to try out.

Elite teams compete in regional tournaments. Because tournament and team costs vary, team fees vary by team and by season.

Who Can Try Out

Open Across the Columbia Basin

MLBA Elite is open to strong players from Moses Lake and surrounding communities. The most common route is through the Academy or the Academy + Team pathway, which includes an invitation to Elite tryouts.

Tryout dates and locations are announced each season and are to be confirmed. Express your interest and we'll follow up with details.
