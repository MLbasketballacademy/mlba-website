How It Works

The Academy + Team Pathway

The complete route: develop in the Academy, try out for Elite, and compete either way.

Train in the Academy

Full Academy access builds fundamentals, confidence, and basketball IQ across the season.

Try Out for Elite

Academy participants receive an invitation to try out for MLBA Elite.

Selected → Elite

Athletes selected join MLBA Elite and pay the team-specific costs.

Not Selected → League

Athletes not selected are placed on a Columbia Basin league team and keep competing.
